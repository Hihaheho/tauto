indexed_refl
Assume n: Nat, witness: Indexed(n).
We prove n = n.

By cases on witness, prove n = n.
  Indexed::AtZero: goal: Zero = Zero.
    By reflexivity, Zero = Zero holds.

  Indexed::AtSucc: assume [k: Nat]; goal: Succ(k) = Succ(k).
    By reflexivity, Succ(k) = Succ(k) holds.

These branches establish n = n.
