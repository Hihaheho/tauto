branches
Assume A: Prop, B: Prop, C: Prop, h: (A) -> B, f: (B) -> C, g: (B) -> C.
We prove And((A) -> C, (A) -> C).

Suppose p: A.
  [1] Using f(h(p)), we obtain C.

[2] By [1], discharging p, we obtain (A) -> C.

Suppose p: A.
  [3] Using g(h(p)), we obtain C.

[4] By [3], discharging p, we obtain (A) -> C.

The conditions are (A) -> C ([2]); (A) -> C ([4]). Applying And::AndIntro proves And((A) -> C, (A) -> C).
